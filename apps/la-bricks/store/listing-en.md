## Full description (max. 4000 characters)

```
LA-Bricks is breakout the way an arcade cabinet would have it: black
background, neon light, one finger, nothing in the way.

Launch the ball, break the wall, don't drop the paddle. That is the whole
rule set — which is why you are playing three seconds after you open it.


THE GAME

All 15 levels are hand-built, not shuffled together: pyramid, checkerboard,
fortress, cross. Steel bricks cannot be destroyed, only played around.
Coloured bricks take two or three hits.

That is not the end of it. In Endless mode the game keeps building levels
itself — always symmetrical, always solvable, a little tougher each time.


SIX POWER-UPS

  MULTIBALL        One ball becomes three
  WIDE PADDLE      More surface, less sweat
  LASER            Shoot your way through the wall
  SLOW MOTION      The ball slows down, you don't
  EXTRA LIFE       One more go
  NARROW PADDLE    The one you should let fall


THREE MODES

  CLASSIC    Three lives, normal speed. The way in.
  ENDLESS    Five lives, more power-ups, calmer pace.
  PRO        One life. Fast ball. Almost no power-ups.


CONTROLS

Swipe to move the paddle. Tap to launch the ball — and later to fire the
laser. That was the complete manual.


PICK YOUR SOUND

Three timbres, all synthesised on the device: LASER (sharp, with a sweep),
RETRO (square waves like early handhelds) or PURE (soft sine tones). Plus
three levels of haptics — or none at all.


WHAT IS NOT IN IT

  No ads. No banners, no videos, no waiting screens.
  No in-app purchases. The price is on the label, nothing follows.
  No account. No sign-up, no email, no login.
  No tracking. No analytics, no ad SDKs, no crash reporting.
  No connection needed. After the first launch everything runs offline.
  No permissions. No location, no contacts, no camera.

Your score, your progress and your settings stay on the device. They do not
leave it.


WHO IT IS FOR

For anyone who wants five minutes of something on the train, in a queue or
on the sofa — something that starts immediately and asks nothing of them.
Portrait, one hand, pause any time.


Good luck with the wall.
```

## Content rating (IARC questionnaire)

Every question on violence, language, sexuality, drugs, gambling and
user-generated content is answered **No**. There is no user interaction, no
location sharing and no sharing of personal data. Expected outcome:
**PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone / rated for all ages**.

## Data safety form

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Does the app collect user data? | No |
| Does the app share user data? | No |
| Is data encrypted in transit? | Not applicable — no data is transmitted |
| Can users request deletion? | Not applicable — no data is collected |

Score, level progress and settings live only in the device's web storage
(`localStorage`) and are never transmitted. Under Google's definition this
does **not** count as collection, because the data never leaves the device.
